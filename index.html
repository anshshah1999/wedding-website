The Ones Getting Married

Meet the Couple

✦

The Bride

Bhavya Gupta

Daughter of Smt. Sadhana and Shri Vikas Gupta

The Heart & The Lens

Bhavya is the vibrant soul of this duo. A tech-savvy computer engineer with an MBA, she has a rare gift for turning ordinary moments into grand memories. Whether she's scouting the city's best hidden culinary gems or ensuring every milestone is celebrated with a flourish, she brings the "extra" to extraordinary.

  • The Foodie ScoutIf there's a new dessert spot or a legendary meal to be found, Bhavya has already bookmarked it.
  • Curator of MomentsShe's the woman behind the camera, capturing the aesthetic, the food, and — most importantly — the quiet smiles Ansh thinks no one sees.
  • The Life of the PartyUnlike Ansh, Bhavya lives for the dance floor. She's the one who reminds us that life is meant to be celebrated loudly, joyfully, and with plenty of dancing.

The Groom

Ansh Shah

Son of Late Smt. Punita and Shri Sushil Shah

The Strategist & The Driver

Ansh is the "old soul" of the pair — a designer by trade and a minimalist at heart. While he might be shy in front of a camera, he's the one behind the scenes making sure the road trips are planned, the driving is smooth, and the strategy is sound. He believes the best nights are spent with close friends, a complex board game, and a very long sleep the next day.

  • The Board Game KingAnsh loves a deep strategy game. He's even curated a special collection of "light" two-player games just so he and Bhavya can share his favourite hobby together.
  • Night Drive EnthusiastFrom Powai to South Bombay, Ansh is always up for a 20 km drive — especially if it's a quiet midnight run to find the perfect paan for Bhavya.
  • The Sweet ToothWhile Bhavya picks the restaurants, Ansh ensures there is always a stop for something sweet. He may be a minimalist, but he never skimps on dessert.

✦ A Note on the "Middle Path" ✦

We are two halves of a very different whole. One prefers the quiet of a strategic game; the other, the energy of a crowded club. But in the middle, we've found our own rhythm: long drives to quiet villas, deep "future" talks over aesthetic dinners, and a love that bridges the gap between Bangalore and Mumbai.

How It All Began

Our Story

✦

Eight years, four cities, and a lifetime of memories — this is the story of how two people found their forever in each other.

Chapter I

The Coaching Class Chronicles

It all began amidst the intense world of IIT entrance coaching in Mumbai. Between complex equations and shared notes, a friendship was quietly blooming. We weren't just classmates; we were each other's steady support system in the whirlwind of 11th and 12th grade.

2014–2016
2016–2018

Chapter II

A Pivot to Design (The Spark)

While Ansh moved on to IIT Bombay to pursue his passion for design, Bhavya found herself drawn to the same creative world. During his first year, Ansh took on a new role: tutor. As he helped Bhavya prepare for her design entrance exams, the "study sessions" slowly transformed into something much deeper. That's when we realized our journey together was just beginning.

Chapter III

Miles, Flights, and FaceTime

Our relationship has always been a testament to the fact that distance is just a number. Across eight years, our story unfolded across four cities and three very different chapters:

  • Bhavya moved to Jaipur for Computer Engineering while Ansh remained in Mumbai — separated by hundreds of kilometres, connected by late-night calls.
  • After a brief reunion in Mumbai (and some very creative "excuses" to see each other during the COVID lockdowns!), Bhavya headed to Hyderabad for her MBA while Ansh pursued his Masters in Mumbai.
  • Just as life brought Bhavya back home to Mumbai for work, Ansh's career path led him to Bangalore — another city, the same love.
2018–2024
2024

Chapter IV · The Grand Finale

The Proposal: A Grand Gesture at Sea

After eight years of long-distance calls and quiet reunions, we knew we were heading toward "forever." Under the guise of an early birthday celebration, and with a little "undercover" help from family, we headed to the iconic Gateway of India. Waiting for us was a private yacht, transformed into a floating sanctuary of flickering candles, soft florals, and a spread of Bhavya's favorite macaroons and tarts.


As the Mumbai skyline glowed in the distance and a curated playlist of "our songs" began to play, Ansh got down on one knee. Against the backdrop of the Arabian Sea, the answer was a resounding "Yes."

The Carnival Treats

Sun-kissed, fragrant, and joyful — just like the day itself. Expect a spread of light bites, chaat stations, and fresh flavours that pair perfectly with marigolds, music, and mehndi.

Sangeet

Wedding
